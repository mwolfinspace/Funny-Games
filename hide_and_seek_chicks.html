🐣 How to Play

🎮 Game Modes

Count Mode: Chicks wander around freely. When the timer ends, they run to colored zones. Count how many are in each zone!

Hide Mode: Chicks gather at flowers and hide. Watch carefully as they switch places! When the timer ends, tap flowers to reveal them.

🕹️ Controls

  • ▶ Start: Begin the round.
  • ⏸ Pause: Freeze the game.
  • 🔄 Reset: Restart the round.
  • 🎮/🌿 Mode: Switch game modes.
  • 🌑 Black Mode: Toggle black chicks.
  • 🐤/⚡/⏱: Adjust chicks, speed, timer.
  • 🌼/🎨: Adjust hiding spots or zones.
  • 🔍 Auto: Toggle auto-zoom.

📊 Status Bar

Click items at the bottom to hide/show their values (useful for hiding the timer or chick count from students!).
