❓ How to Play

Goal: Use friendly ships (outside grid) to shoot lasers. Lasers bounce off shields, hit ships, or exit the grid. Predict behavior to win.

Laser Rules

  • Each enemy ship has a circular shield (radius covers 3×3 area).
  • Direct hit: laser enters the cell directly facing the ship → damages ship, stops.
  • 90° reflect: laser enters a diagonal cell of the 3×3 → bounces sideways.
  • 180° back: laser hits the intersection of two overlapping shields → returns to source.
  • Boundary rule: shots engaging a boundary/corner ship's shield from OUTSIDE the grid (through the bulge beyond the edges) reflect straight back (180°); beams engaging from inside the map follow the normal rules above.

Game Modes

  • Detect Out: place flags where you think lasers will exit or hit.
  • Find Enemy: enemies are hidden. Use laser behavior to deduce positions, then flag them.
  • Read Map: results shown at edges (Hit / ↩ / numbered pairs sharing a color). Flag enemy positions.

Tools

  • Click cells with the active tool to place/remove items. Right-click quickly erases any ship or flag.
  • Quick Play generates a random setup based on grid & ship counts.
  • The Beam slider sets the lit portion of the route (%): beams stream from start to end in a continuous loop, like trains on a track. Lasers always reach their exact endpoint. Speed sets velocity.
