Over 400+ everyday English phrases for kids and learners (like "LET THE GAME BEGIN", "GO TO THE COFFEE SHOP", or "GOOD MORNING MY FRIEND") are hidden inside a square letter grid. Place a perforated stencil mask over the grid and turn it 90° four times to reveal the secret sentence!

🧭 HOW TO PLAY & CREATE CHALLENGES:
  1. Step 1 — Pick or Build a Hole Grid: Click a card in the Bank of Decode Grids, OR design your own stencil in the Hole Grid (bottom left) and click ⬆️ Place.
  2. Step 2 — Watch Rotation Direction: Click ↻ 90° (clockwise) or ↺ 90° (counter-clockwise). Turning in the wrong direction scrambles the word order and will not pass!
  3. Step 3 — Collect All 4 Turns & Check: After 4 turns (T1 → T2 → T3 → T4), click ✅ Check. Any recognized English phrase or matching target sentence wins!
  4. Ultra-Short Seed ID Mode (🗑️ Clear All): Remove individual bank cards with ✖ or click 🗑️ Clear All to leave only the Cipher Grid! Your 🔑 Seed ID shrinks to just a few characters, challenging players to deduce the Hole Grid themselves!
  5. Custom Challenge Creator (✍️ Make Puzzle): Type any English sentence, choose ↻ CW or ↺ CCW encryption direction, and share the compact Seed ID or 📐 SVG cutouts!
